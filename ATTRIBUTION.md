## Sommelier producer bottle photographs

The 25 Sommelier wine styles include **representative real producer bottle photographs**, linked from external wine retailers and catalogs. Each entry names its producer and product and contains the image source page and credit in `data/sommelier-wines.json`; the live wine detail provides a link to that source. Illustrations made for Cocktail Atlas remain the fallback.
